## Store listing (English)

**Product name:** Images App

**Short description** (shown in some views, ≤ 270 characters):

**Description:**

> Images is a fast, minimal photo viewer for Windows. It looks and feels like the built-in Photos app, but opens your pictures instantly.
>
> • Opens photos in about 100 ms. Neighbors are preloaded, so flipping through a folder is instant.
> • Smooth pan and zoom: wheel zoom at the cursor, drag to pan, double-click to fit or show actual size, and a persistent Fit to window mode.
> • Browse with the arrow keys in the same order as File Explorer.
> • Info pane with dimensions, file size, date taken, camera, lens and exposure details.
> • Supports every format Windows has a codec for: JPEG, PNG, GIF, WebP, HEIC, AVIF, TIFF and camera RAW files. Applies EXIF orientation and color profiles.
> • Fits right into Windows 11: light and dark themes, Snap Layouts, touch gestures, high contrast and text scaling.
> • Copy an image with Ctrl+C, delete it to the Recycle Bin, or go full screen with F11.
> • No AI features, no cloud, no accounts, no telemetry. Your photos never leave your PC.
> • Tiny: under 1 MB.
